# World list (as of 2026-01-05)

What the reader runs, depends on and works on. The new tab tags each story with
a "→ label" chip when one of a label's terms appears in it, and the model uses
the list to decide whether a story "touches" you. EXAMPLE CONTENT: replace every
line with your own.

Format: `## Group` headings; under each, one bullet per thing:
`- Label: term, term, term`. Terms match case-insensitively as whole words.
Notes in parentheses are ignored. `[slug]` after a label names a project folder
your assistant knows; only labels with a slug can start a security-advisory
session.
